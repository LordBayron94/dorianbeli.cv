About

I am a Data Engineer with a multifaceted skill set encompassing the end-to-end data lifecycle. I am experienced with on-premise and AWS cloud architecture, and interested in applying that knowledge in hybrid architectures.

I am proficient in developing, integrating, testing and troubleshooting solutions, and my competencies in DevOps practices — CI/CD integrations and Linux command-line work — ensure seamless and efficient operations.

Alongside my engineering work, I am a doctoral researcher at the University of Turku, Department of Computing, researching context-aware OCR and handwritten text recognition (HTR) for historical documents — using vision-language models to read archival material faithfully while staying accountable to visual evidence, document structure and historical context. It continues a thread that runs through my work: from an industrial OCR pipeline built at TU Graz to a master's thesis on NLP-based text summarization.
