About

I am a doctoral researcher at the Department of Computing, University of Turku, researching context-aware OCR and handwritten text recognition (HTR) for historical documents — using vision-language models to read archival material faithfully, transparently and reproducibly, while staying accountable to visual evidence, document structure and historical context.

I came to research from industry: several years as a data engineer with a multifaceted skill set encompassing the end-to-end data lifecycle — on-premise and AWS cloud architecture, hybrid architectures, and DevOps practices such as CI/CD integrations and Linux command-line work. I am proficient in developing, integrating, testing and troubleshooting data solutions.

The research continues a thread that runs through my career: from an industrial OCR pipeline built at TU Graz to a master's thesis on NLP-based text summarization.
